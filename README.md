# Yellowstone: The Trophic Cascade

An interactive narrative simulation about the reintroduction of wolves 
to Yellowstone National Park and the ecological cascade that followed.

## About
Built for [Course Name] — [Your Name]

This simulation walks through the history of wolf extermination in the 
1920s, the ecological collapse that followed, and the dramatic recovery 
after wolves were reintroduced in 1995.

## How to Play
- Click anywhere on the screen to advance the story
- When prompted, choose how many wolves to reintroduce
- Use the sliders on the final screen to explore what happens when 
  you change wolf and human hunting pressure
- Click any animal to learn about its role in the ecosystem
